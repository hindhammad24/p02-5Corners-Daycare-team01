Technology

We use technology to help families stay connected, informed, and engaged throughout the day.

Brightwheel

At 5 Corners Daycare, we use Brightwheel to make communication with families easier, faster, and more convenient.

Brightwheel helps parents stay connected with their child’s daycare experience in one place. Families can receive important messages, updates, and helpful information throughout the day.

Through Brightwheel, we can support better parent communication, simplify check-ins, and share updates in a way that is easy to access and family-friendly.

We believe technology should make your experience smoother while keeping you connected to your child’s care, learning, and daily routine.
